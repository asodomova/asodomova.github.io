Hauntologie ztracených zítřků

Essay · Published in Advojka, 2025 · Czech

Hauntology of Lost Tomorrows. Originally written for Advojka, this essay traces how the futures we were promised in the late twentieth century — the gentle, social-democratic ones, the technologically curious ones — came to feel less like options and more like ghosts. Not gone, exactly. Just unable to fully arrive.

Borrowing Mark Fisher's reading of Derrida, the piece argues that hauntology in the Czech context has a particular shape: it is doubled. We are haunted both by the futures of the West we never got to inhabit, and by the futures our own state-socialist past gestured at but could not deliver. Two ghosts, talking past each other, in the same hallway.
