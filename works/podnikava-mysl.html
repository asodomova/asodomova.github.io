Podnikavá mysl [Enterprising mind]

NGO visual identity · 2021

Full visual identity for a Czech non-profit running entrepreneurship workshops in regional schools. Wordmark, palette, a small library of editorial illustrations, and templates that the team could keep using without me.

The brief was unusually well-written: "Make us look like a school newspaper that takes itself a little too seriously." I tried to honour that.
