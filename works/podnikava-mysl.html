Podnikavá mysl, visual identity & web

I worked on the visual identity of the Podnikavá mysl (Enterprising mind) in the second half of 2021 through collaboration with the Brno studio Steezy.

At that time, Podnikavá mysl was a newly established, at the time the sixth workplace of the environmental education organization Lipka. Its goal was to support entrepreneurship in schools across the South Moravian Region, and Entrepreneurial Mind specifically served as a platform to connect educators in pursuit of this goal. I worked on the project as a brand identity designer — I designed the logo Podnikavá mysl and prepared a graphic manual for its use. In cooperation with my colleagues, we designed and supplied the organisation with business cards, presentation template, letterhead paper and the final piece was the website, which we also made work internally.

Our work was preceded by a workshop where the client clarified their expectations and philosophy behind the Podnikavá mysl brand.
