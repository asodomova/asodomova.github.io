Motivation Labs, complete brand redesign & web development

A full brand redesign for a small organizational psychology company I used to work at part time during my master's studies – as a psychologist, which was however also happy to dust off and use my graphic design past to help with a long planned rebrand.

Redesign was based on a small number of focus groups, which aside from the visual rebrand concerned both the company's future development & solidifying the vision of what Motivation Labs is and what it stands for. It was my second web design project.
