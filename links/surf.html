I want to surf the web

I want to surf the web — not consume it, not graze it, not feed it. Surfing implies a wave, an effort, an unpredictable shape under your feet. The current web does not surf well; it scrolls.

This page is here mostly to remind me that the verb existed once, and that the activity it described is still possible if you go looking for it. Webrings, RSS, link blogs, mailing lists with three subscribers. The sea is still there. You just have to walk further out.
