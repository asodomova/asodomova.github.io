My tierlist of academic personal websites

The criteria are unscientific: does the page load fast, does the writing feel personal, can I find a paper without rage-clicking. Tier S is reserved for sites that look like they were made by the same person who writes the work.

S
  • Maciej Cegłowski's Idle Words
  • Robin Sloan's homepage
  • Frank Chimero, when it existed
A
  • Most of the IAS Princeton folks
  • Cosma Shalizi's notebooks
B
  • Anyone whose site is just a Google Scholar page in disguise
F
  • Departmental templates with a stock photo of a microscope
