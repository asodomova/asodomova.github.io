Reading: Calvino, Six Memos for the Next Millennium

Calvino died before he could deliver the sixth lecture. Five memos: lightness, quickness, exactitude, visibility, multiplicity. The missing one would have been consistency, and I think about that absence more than the five he managed to write down.

This page exists so I can dump notes here as I work through the book again. Check back if you're into that kind of thing.
